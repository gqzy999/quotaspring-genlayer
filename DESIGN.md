# Design and adjacent mechanisms

The bounded repository review on 2026-10-29 covered accessible gqzy999, chuanqili77, zuotu295, chongqianli7 and zsf11221 repositories, plus accessible public gqzy1 and halihalibt repositories. It does not establish worldwide novelty.

The semantic layer handles only the explicit cap in the fixed unit. Validators independently regenerate all caps. Withholding the budget from the model limits a direct route for outcome-driven extraction. It does not make the model correct or a declaration truthful.

The deterministic layer progressively saturates caps. Among unsaturated members, every final share is the same exact level. This is the unique capped equal-weight max-min allocation when total use is maximal. If all caps are satisfied, excess budget remains unallocated. Fraction pairs avoid floating-point disagreement and member-order remainder favoritism. Permuting member order permutes the shares without changing their values.

Each wallet must submit and claim for itself. A claim is an immutable internal receipt, not delivery or a transferable token. Integrators must handle fractions and authenticate full final state; there is no external resource service in this repository.
